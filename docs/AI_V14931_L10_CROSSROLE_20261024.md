# Arcane Duel – v14.9.31: Hard-Level-10 Cross-Role AI Benchmark

## Verified reason for this test
Successful v14.9.30 run #40031690133 simulated 4,800 two-sided
strategic test-AI duels. Four challengers scored more HOLDOUT wins
than the prior Guardian/Soul champion (153 wins), notably
`enemy-role-aware` (176) and `role-economy` (172). Yet none passed
the predefined TRAIN+VALIDATION global promotion gate; role-economy
lost nine TRAIN wins on the first candidate seat, and the L10
cross-role HOLDOUT matched champion at 34/64 for all candidates.
No promotion occurred and no previous HOLDOUT metrics have been used
to tune this run dynamically; previous findings only motivate fixed
hypotheses that must be tested on NEW seeds.

## Fixed new strategy arms
1. `guardian-champion` – unchanged v14.9.28 validated incumbent
2. `enemy-role-aware` – preserve strong previous visible-role heuristic
3. `role-economy` – preserve material conservation role heuristic
4. `role-level10-guard` – explicit observable high-level monster threat
   adjusts Guardian summoning and attack risk
5. `role-economy-l10` – combines material retention, enemy-role
   assessment, and revealed level-9/10 monster counter-threats

No new live card rules, hand-knowledge or hidden opponent deck facts.
These are deterministic research-only scoring adjustments in the
existing full two-sided test AI. No claims of neural or end-to-end
reinforcement learning.

## Curriculum and opponent diversity
Five test phases, 960 games each:
- Lv8 identical-deck mirrors
- Lv9 identical-deck mirrors
- Lv10 cross-role: mixed Predator, Fusion, Hybrid, Guardian, Soul,
  and Allrounder opponents
- Lv8 cross-role with diverse opposing roles
- Lv10 hard cross-role: challenging Predator/Fusion/Guardian/Hybrid
  pairings

Both seats are operated by the full test AI planner. Opponent
checkpoint scenarios alternate between the verified v14.9.28
Guardian/Soul champion and v14.9.29 balanced research policy,
with correct historical-Q settings. Every challenger and incumbent
faces identical seeds, decks, seat and starter assignments.

The model and game seeds are new; a built-in seed upper bound
check prevents exceeding the benchmark deck generator's 9999 limit.

## Splits, training transfer, promotion policy
Across 4,800 matches: 1,600 TRAIN; 1,600 distinct VALIDATION;
1,600 untouched HOLDOUT. Each fixed strategy plays 320 matches
per split, and 64 per phase and split.

Evaluate global candidates only after all 5 stages on TRAIN+
VALIDATION. Promotion requires:
- at least 8 extra TRAIN wins and 8 extra VALIDATION wins vs champion;
- neither seat performs worse in TRAIN or VALIDATION;
- no more than 3 fewer wins in mirror, cross-role, champion-opponent
  or balanced-opponent VALIDATION groups;
- additionally **no regression in hardest Level-10 Cross-Role
  VALIDATION stage (stage 4)**.

Only after candidate selection may a report inspect the HOLDOUT.
If no challenger passes all gates, retain the Guardian/Soul champion.

## Historical learning and reliability
Carry forward latest accepted tactical Q from successful v14.9.30
and 20 successful historical TRAIN-only archives. All tactical Q
changes remain subject to independent checks.
Use 40 separate Node processes (8×120 per stage) and fail-closed
checks on all expected duels, forbidden stance changes, both player
planner usage and paired seeds.

Arena LP decay remains turned off symmetrically for the research
benchmarks. Full live-rule validity is not yet proven.
